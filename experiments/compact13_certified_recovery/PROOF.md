# Two-location certificate for the 298-output compact13 schedule with a recovery veto

**Theorem.** For CertifiedCompact with the options in certify.py, every accepted output under a shared budget of at most two faulty elementary locations is a linear combination of physical errors of weight at most two acting on the target CZ magic state, allowing arbitrary environment coefficients. Ideal recovery returns the target. This uses the [tree4 model](../tree4_flag_repair/PROOF.md) of arbitrary participant-local faults, single-wire waits and reliable classical control. Retries consume the same budget. The original HookCompact and its archived data remain available in the sibling comparison directory.

## The gap and the change

In a full unflagged reference, the original first consults the data syndrome over all possible pairs of inner blocks. If that is inconclusive it uses the measured cycle word to restrict the candidate block families. Checking the latter table against the actual compact13 graph preparation reveals a missing combination. An X on vertex a1 immediately after CX(a1,A4) propagates into later incident chords A5, A6 and A10. Their cycle word is 70. In diagnostic coordinates the vertex flip relative to the four pre-existing tree bits introduces the block operator on block 1. A second fault can act on block 0. The old families for cycle 70 contain only the empty support and singleton blocks, omitting the pair (0,1). The missing family invalidates a direct appeal to that lookup table's coverage.

On Born seed 0 this vertex fault and an X on helper s after CX(s,D1) in the first ordinary inner-X vote actually reach the narrowed decision. The old lookup proposes the data mask (3,0). The original controller eventually rejects for insufficient votes. The guarded variant rejects the narrowed choice immediately. The execution establishes reachability of the decision; whether the original can produce an incorrect accepted output remains open. [Injected faults and single-fault controls](witness.json)

CertifiedCompact vetoes precisely a proposed cycle-only **data** correction in this fallback. Every other decision, including flagged recovery, data-unique corrections, existing rejections, cycle/chord feedback, physical checks, slot counts and final screen, follows the original code. The veto is a sufficient guard, with no claim that each vetoed record is individually unsafe. It adds no quantum operation. [Implementation](certify.py)

## Composition

Write L for the encoded span of the prepared ++ state and its transversal-U image; the positive eigenspace in L is the target line M. B_f(J) allows arbitrary errors on at most f four-qubit blocks on J, with arbitrary graph/environment coefficients. Use the diagnostic coordinate change V controlled only by the four tree edges. While physically gauged, assertions about the data below refer to these coordinates.

1. **Source, thirteen-edge resource, and entry.** The screened source is the original certified source. The [compact13 circuit audit](../compact13_deciding_vote/checks.json) checks the actual graph prefix including the nine chord preparations and the targeted tree flag. The vertex/tree-bit defect modulo complementary labels has weight at most the number of prefix faults for every surviving one- and two-location Pauli term. Chord phases affect the coefficients. Clean complementary vertex words produce identical chords, giving the ideal signed-U projector at entry. Enrichment and feedback faults charge their individual blocks. This yields B_f(L), or B_f(M) after a clean positive entry. The [compact13 block-invariant argument](../compact13_deciding_vote/PROOF.md) applies before the policy for deciding votes diverges.
2. **Ordinary votes.** Here *every* post-entry vote, including a deciding vote, has the original nine Z and five inner-X sign checks or their recovery branch. The five graph stars multiply to U since every edge occurs twice. In diagnostic coordinates the nonroot stars act on the graph, and the root is U times a graph star. A nonroot fault involves at most its addressed block and chords; the compact root's zero-flag protected-port bound includes cancelling flag pairs. The [emitted-circuit audit](../compact13_deciding_vote/audit.py) checks the root with chords. The existing bare-inner, outer-Z, and Z-only recovery lemmas hold with spectator graph wires. On a clean vote the Z reference removes the X component up to stabilizers; the five inner-X bits determine any remaining Z sign flip. A full clean recovery restores the code directly. A clean positive thus selects M. A dirty instrument preserves the underlying L or M with the charged block support. [Sign frame and meter argument](../tree4_flag_repair/PROOF.md)
3. **Full references.** A triggering nonzero Z syndrome or flag requires an already-spent fault on this ideal input, leaving at most one new fault. The coordinate passes are disjoint block/tree-edge components. Between the passes, ordinary data CSS measurements commute with all graph cycle/star measurements, and stars commute with cycles. The two matching complete rows have a clean occurrence. At that occurrence each data Pauli term has the selected syndrome. Graph projections can select coefficients but cannot add data support. Later accepted zero-flag conditional data meters add at most one site per remaining fault modulo their measured generator. Graph meter faults between the passes are graph-only in these coordinates. Feedback is relative to the clean occurrence: the known Pauli commutes through later Pauli tails up to phase. A fault in returning through V costs at most its block.
   * For an unflagged accepted correction, the guard now guarantees the same stabilizer class for **every** error on up to two blocks with the selected data syndrome. Applying it restores the underlying vector at the clean occurrence. An absent or ambiguous class never produces data feedback from cycle evidence alone.
   * For a flagged correction, the original physical flag table and inherited coordinate-aware veto apply to a compact inner data meter. The only possible extra data from other wires is at most one outside block; the two edge-bit conjugates of the flag tail are both covered. Two faults inside a single inner meter stay on that block. This includes the nested inner flag after a Z-only correction. Outer one-flag and root flags reject. Conditional history flags reject. The extra chord coordinates do not enter V or enlarge the inherited flag-tail algebra. [Flagged-reference proof and finite audit](../tree4_flag_repair/PROOF.md)
   * Both cases may still apply X feedback to **chords** to flatten a cycle word, and Z feedback to **tree** edges to restore star signs. These are graph operations in the reference coordinates; the data guarantee holds for arbitrary graph coefficients. The ideal outgoing V restores the physical gauged representation. Extra data or tree-edge faults in executing feedback or V charge their own components. No cycle-to-block localization assumption is used here.
4. **Acceptance and handoff.** Three positive slots under two faults include a clean positive. Subsequent dirty votes preserve M with the appropriate block charge. Reading the edges and applying feedback from the four tree bits cancels V on each true computational branch; a faulty tree report introduces at most its own block operator. Chord reports do not select data feedback. The global budget therefore supplies B_f(M) at the ordered terminal screen. The [terminal replacement lemma](../../docs/single-block-cz/components/single_block_cz_terminal_screen.md) converts that input on every accepted branch to a physical error of weight at most two, including the remaining screen, fallback, and final boundary faults, term by term. The distance-six code corrects this span.

## Reproduction and scope of the measured cost

Run certify.py, then summarize.py, and run witnesses.py for the targeted two-fault replay. The cohort runner creates its ledger exclusively. The [summary](summary.json) compares all 500 seeds with the archived original, including per-seed accept/reject reason, decoded diagnostics, physical gate counts, sampled noise counts and peak live qubits. Four seeds used the ambiguous-data fallback, whose joint lookup also declined a correction on all four. All 298 accepted outputs and all T counts were reproduced. An application on a different seed or noise model can reach the new veto, so equal yield and cost are empirical properties of this cohort.
