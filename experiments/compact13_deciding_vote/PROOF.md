# Reusing the ordered terminal screen for compact13's deciding vote

**Claim.** For DeferredCompact and RecoveringCompact in this directory, in the participant-local circuit model of the [tree4 theorem](../tree4_flag_repair/PROOF.md), every accepting branch with at most two faulty elementary locations across the attempt, including retries, is in the span of physical weight-at-most-two errors on the encoded target. This gives unit fidelity under ideal decoding. Classical records and control are reliable. The claim includes arbitrary local quantum faults correlated with an environment. The stochastic experiment allows any number of faults.

**Schedule.** The first two positive votes use the existing sign frame. Once there are two positives, every subsequent slot measures just the four nonroot Gauss observables and the root Gauss observable. A raw negative consumes a slot. A raw positive leads to destructive handoff and the ordered data screen. The accepting deciding slot includes this entire tail, its recovery and final waits. DeferredCompact accepts only a zero screen. RecoveringCompact also admits ordinary verified flag recovery and certain verified syndrome corrections, as specified below. Both reject a request for the full *gauged* reference rather than using the unproved compact13 cycle-conditioned correction. The unambiguous Z-only gauged recovery remains available.

## Block-error invariant before screening

Use the tree4 proof's diagnostic coordinates V controlled by the four tree edges, logical sign span L, positive magic line M, and spaces B_f(J) of errors on at most f inner blocks on J with arbitrary graph/environment coefficients. The source, ordinary bare inner checks, one-flag outer Z checks and unambiguous Z-only recovery are the [certified tree/cat instruments](../tree4_cat_fairness/PROOF.md). Their action on data and the four controlling tree edges is identical. Waiting chord faults in these instruments act on the graph. Any full gauged reference call rejects. The terminal recovery is separate and in original data coordinates.

**Preparing all thirteen edges.** For a computational branch with vertex bits t and tree bits w, the entry defect on leaf v is w_v XOR t_0 XOR t_v. Its controlled data action is the four-site block product on each defective leaf, modulo global U. Only tree bits enter V. The exact Clifford propagation of the emitted graph prefix (including chord preparation, the helper report, and live-wire waits) bounds the smaller defect modulo complement by one for an accepted single fault and by two for every flag-cancelling pair. Resource phases and chord words may change the coefficients of these terms arbitrarily. This extends to Kraus components by Pauli expansion. Failed tree resources precede data enrichment; waiting data faults remain charged. On clean entry t and its complement give identical values on *every* edge, since edges store endpoint XORs. Their vertex-X reports therefore implement the two projectors of U exactly as for tree4. Enrichment and handoff have the same one-block spread per fault. The handoff uses the four reported tree bits, and measures the other nine edges without using them to correct data.

**Gauss instruments.** In diagnostic coordinates a clean nonroot observable is its graph X star. The root observable is U times its graph X star. Every edge appears twice in the product, so their joint sign measures U, irrespective of cycle syndromes or graph amplitudes. Resolving the individual commuting star reports introduces extra graph projectors, all commuting with data U. A dirty nonroot meter touches its own data block and its one controlling tree edge; its other edges are chords, which commute with V. Thus arbitrary faults there can charge only that data block, in addition to spectator faults on their own wires. The root is the protected eight-port tree skeleton followed by chord couplings. The emitted Clifford skeleton, retaining the eight protected ports and testing the actual four flag bits, has zero-flag support at most one or two ports respectively for one or two faults, modulo the product on those eight ports. The product differs from the full measured product only on chords, which can be absorbed into the graph coefficient. The signed local rotations each bracket just one data CX, so their faults are covered by the same local-port expansion used in the tree proof. Multiplying by U preserves both underlying spaces and conjugates an inherited error within its original support. This proves dirty-slot closure on B_f(L) and B_f(M), including skipped data checks and negative deciding attempts. A clean ordinary positive selects underlying M by the nine-Z/five-X sign-frame lemma. The source and entry together count as the entry slot.

## Why the last data check can be deferred

Let P be the joint original-code projector, and let P+ be the positive projector of U. They commute. Conjugating an even Z generator by U preserves it. Conjugating an X generator gives the same X times its matching Z generator with positive sign (the actual generator weights are multiples of four with even negative-sign counts). Moreover, for any Pauli E on at most two inner blocks, P E P is scalar on the code: a nonzero syndrome is killed, and at zero syndrome both CSS components are stabilizers. The audit checks the CSS kernel over all ten block pairs. Consequently P maps B_2(L) into L, term by term.

Three positives in different slots imply that at least one is clean. There are two cases.

1. **An earlier positive is clean.** Entry or an ordinary framed vote has already established M. All later Gauss slots preserve its block envelope, including unframed votes of either sign. Wrong tree reports and faulty byproduct gates charge their own blocks through handoff. The inherited ordered-screen theorem converts this envelope to physical site weight at most two on every admitted terminal branch, including remaining boundary faults. Rejecting some recovery records preserves the claim.
2. **Only the accepting deciding slot is clean.** The two previous positives consumed both faults. In diagnostic coordinates the raw-positive Gauss branch lies in the positive U eigenspace, and remains in B_2(L). On each measured edge word clean handoff cancels V exactly. The clean all-zero ordered screen applies P. The result is in L and still has positive U sign, so it is in M. This argument permits arbitrary inherited two-block errors, including X components, without reading the omitted pre-vote syndrome. It also applies if additional negative slots intervened.

For RecoveringCompact, a **first-screen flag** cannot occur in case 2: a clean compact flag instrument returns zero flags on every input. Case 1 therefore covers every accepted first-flag recovery branch. For a **nonzero first syndrome** in case 2 the clean complete reference projects onto a syndrome sector P_s. The preceding partial screen commutes with the reference, so it gives the same sector on each surviving term. The controller requires a unique applied correction C and admits only pure Z masks of even weight (including identity). Thus C commutes with U, and P_s = C† P C also commutes with U. The clean reference and correction retain the positive sign. Uniqueness of the two-block syndrome class implies C P_s maps B_2(L) into L: each term with syndrome s differs from C by a stabilizer and is restored. The corrected output is consequently in M. For a dirty deciding slot the earlier-clean case already covers terminal recovery. No inference that a raw positive alone is a logical certificate is needed.
