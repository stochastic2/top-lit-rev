# Two-location guarantees for the audited cat and tree4 arms

The eight holdout controllers have the following accepted-output guarantee. With at most two faulty elementary locations in an attempt, their final data lie in the span of weight-at-most-two physical Paulis on encoded CZ magic. An ideal distance-six decoder therefore returns the target, including for coherent operations and correlated faults. The success probability without faults is 3/4 for every arm. The theorem assumes reliable classical control and the same participant-local preparation, reset, gate, waiting, destructive measurement and report model as the repaired-tree proof. It allows a common quantum environment. Retry tokens, completed votes, reference acquisition and handoff all share the same two locations.

The exact `RepairedTree` uses its existing theorem. Sections 1–6 below address the direct cat, including the exact `ScreenCat` used in the final diagnostic. Section 7 addresses the tree changes and establishes the boundary of the screen relocation. ## 1. State spaces and source

Let $U=\prod_i W_i$ for the actual twenty signed involutions $W_i=T_i^{-s_i}X_iT_i^{s_i}$. Its restriction to the code is logical CZ. Let $L$ be the encoded span of $|++\rangle$ and $U|++\rangle$. The $+$ projector of $U$ maps $L$ onto the magic line $M$. Both $L$ and $M$ are invariant under $U$.

For $J=L,M$, write $B_f(J)$ for the span of $E|\psi\rangle$ with $E$ a Pauli on at most $f$ inner blocks of four sites and $|\psi\rangle\in J$. Write $P_f(J)$ for the corresponding span with at most $f$ physical sites. Coefficients can be arbitrary environment vectors. A fixed support common to every term is unnecessary. Expand each faulty local operator in Paulis on its participants with operator-valued coefficients on the environment. A faulty destructive report can be represented by a conditional bra on the measured wire with such coefficients. Writing each component relative to the reported ideal bra includes a wrong bit combined with arbitrary measurement action at one location.

All arms execute the same screened twelve-check source with a common physical prefix. The surviving zero-screen raw encoder errors obey the fixed-location source certificate. On the nonzero branch, three identical complete reports contain a clean occurrence. The rank-one source sector is restored by the signed right inverse, with at most one block per late fault in accepted meters and feedback. A source flag can rebuild the unused source. The existing source argument then gives $B_f(L)$ after $f$ locations in the source. The source and its retries are charged to the first vote. An honest first vote requires a clean source slot as well as a clean U instrument. ## 2. The physical cat has a site bound

**Preparation and verification.** The actual raw program prepares twenty zero leaves, applies H to L0, then the nineteen chain CXs in order. In the fast checks all couplings are leaf-controlled CX into a fresh zero helper measured in Z. The ideal checks have even support and leave both computational GHZ words unchanged. Push local Pauli terms through this Clifford prefix. Their bit/report signature is $(d,r)$, where $d$ is the leaf-X mask and $r$ is the vector of report flips relative to zero. The surviving terms of one fault have $r=0$. With two faults the two signatures must have equal $r$ to survive. Phases leave the bit/report signatures invariant. They are allowed as arbitrary coefficients of the two cat words.

The exact audit extracts the live circuit from each controller, including preparation and all waiting leaf/helper locations up to the first port. An independent Clifford X/Z propagation gives the same projected signatures as the forward bit/report audit. The tuned five-parity prefix has 1,241 locations and 67 signatures including identity. The four-parity prefix has 959 locations and 54 signatures. For both, each surviving combined bit mask obeys
$$
 \min(|d|,20-|d|)\le f,\qquad f=0,1,2.
$$
A waiting data fault during the prefix is a separate physical site charge. Initial leaf waiting faults before H and the ability of a faulty readout to choose the wrong report are included. The signature-pair enumeration allows same-location pairs as an overestimate and includes identity components.

The four supports are `{0,7,12,16}`, `{1,14}`, `{4,19}`, `{10,18}`. Each leaf is used at most once in the fast parity scan, so a single scan fault affects at most one leaf bit and one parity report. To see the chain construction, the four-bit check syndrome along prefix boundaries 0 through 20 is
`0,1,3,3,3,7,7,7,6,6,6,14,14,15,15,13,13,12,12,4,0`.
A raw X hook is a suffix or a singleton. The dangerous suffixes at boundaries 2 through 18 have syndrome weight at least two. Each group of equal dangerous syndromes occupies at most three adjacent boundaries. Thus two cancelling raw suffixes differ on at most two sites, and a single parity-report error cannot mask a dangerous suffix. The compiled audit checks the complete case split including ports of the parity scan. Four checks and ten verification CX are the construction used here.

**Nonzero fast report.** At least one location was already faulty. The fallback takes full adjacent-ZZ scans until two consecutive records agree, with a four-record cap. At least one of those occurrences is clean. Its projectors select the span of a single word $t$ and its complement. The implemented prefix-X feedback sends this pair to $(0^{20},1^{20})$. A later fault in an adjacent parity meter can change at most its one participating leaf bit, since the helper is always the CX target and is destroyed. A late flip during the second matching row can be invisible to that row and is still charged. A leaf correction is a single-site gate. The surviving cat therefore has the same site bound relative to its actual clean reference, with arbitrary phases and environment coefficients. Deciding from the selected value requires no assumption that the last matching row was clean.

**Interaction and readout.** Every leaf controls exactly one signed physical $T^{s_i},\mathrm{CX},T^{-s_i}$ port. With ideal ports and any conditional leaf bras, a cat component on $d,\bar d$ acts on the input by
$$
 W_d(aI+bU),\qquad W_d=\prod_{i:d_i=1}W_i .
$$
Choosing $\bar d$ instead of $d$ interchanges the two coefficients. This identity permits complementary large correlated hooks and arbitrary phases; it never assumes a faulty cat is GHZ+. A fault on an individual basis rotation or on the CX can be written as a sum of leaf matrix elements with an arbitrary operator on that **one** data target. For each of the two input words, factor the nominal $W_i^{d_i}$ out of the local operator. Factoring independently per port and moving the global $U$ past an inherited error preserves its sites because $U$ is transversal. One faulty port therefore adds at most its target site even when both participants are faulty. A leaf idle before its port can add its own site. A leaf idle after its port, H, or faulty destructive report alters the conditional coefficients. A data idle adds its own site. The exact two-sign port audit checks the actual elementary decomposition. Thus $f$ faults in the cat instrument increase the allowed support by at most $f$ **sites**, preserving underlying $J$ on any reported branch. In a fault-free cat, XOR of the twenty X reports gives the projectors $I\pm U$ up to scalar.

This establishes why correlated cat faults cannot invalidate the shortened sign certificate. A dirty cat vote is allowed any report and only changes the charged support and the two logical-branch coefficients. In any later honest vote—even on an error term produced by two correlated faults in the same earlier cat—the incoming error obeys the two-block hypothesis of the sign argument. A dirty cat after an honest positive vote likewise preserves the underlying magic line. The three-positive rule absorbs arbitrary dirty-vote signs.

## 3. Short checks and recovery under the shared budget

The nine Z checks are measured first. For each inner block, its bare four-site Z meter has no access to any other block. The outer Z meter visits consecutive two-site groups in four blocks with one flag coupling after ports 2 and 6. On zero flags the physical tail is modulo the measured stabilizer on at most one block for one fault and two blocks for cancellation of two flag signatures. Every nonzero flag from this gadget rejects. The subsequent five inner X meters are bare, so their tails are block-confined even on dirty sectors. Ideal original-code Pauli meters on an $E|\psi\rangle$ term select the syndrome of $E$ and preserve its support and its encoded vector. This is true if an incorrect report sends a dirty state down the nominal clean branch.

A nonzero routine Z record requires at least one fault somewhere so far. With at most one fault left, the Z-only fallback measures complete nine-Z rows using dual single-fault flags. Any conditional flag rejects. Two consecutive identical rows contain a clean occurrence. If the two-block table selects a unique correction for this Z syndrome with all X syndrome bits zero, its Z mask is zero. To see why this table is sufficient, the X mask $x$ of each actual error term yields the candidate $(x,0)$ with that syndrome. Uniqueness therefore implies that the selected X mask differs from every actual $x$ only by an X stabilizer. It removes the X part at the clean occurrence without removing the pending Z part. Later faults in zero-flag conditional meters or in the physical X correction add at most one physical site per fault. The controller then reads all five inner-X signs. No extra V pass is needed on cat.

If the Z-only lookup is ambiguous or absent, the controller starts a fresh full recovery. The full recovery uses three matching complete rows, with epoch resets on intervening flag repair. All accepted unflagged meter tails are site-bounded. The table includes every two-block Pauli and only returns a correction on a unique stabilizer class; otherwise it rejects. Apply the recovery relative to an actual clean occurrence and commute its known Pauli correction through later tails. The decoded error becomes the sum of those later physical-site charges. An earlier spent fault is still spent after correction.

For a compact-meter flag, the physical flag conditioner includes each exact tagged tail with one arbitrary outside block and pairs of internal tails with the reported XOR. This covers an earlier persisting term as well as a fault in waiting, reference acquisition or feedback, and includes a false flag. Two equal dual-flag full-code rows after the flag contain a clean occurrence. When that table allows a correction, it removes the complete inherited class at the reference; late zero-flag tails and feedback then add only physical-site charges. All these flags are in original data coordinates. There is no edge-one conjugation in a cat. A new conditional flag rejects, and the controller keeps the existing finite caps. ## 4. Honest positives are logical certificates

After nine honest positive Z checks a surviving Pauli on at most two blocks has an X mask that is a product of full-block X stabilizers. The exact kernel check covers every pair and shows that no proper subset of the original nine checks has this property on all block pairs. Up to phase on every encoded vector the error is $Z^z$. The five measured inner Xs multiply to all twenty Xs, so the XOR of their reported negative bits is $p=|z|\bmod 2$. Each $W_i$ anticommutes with $Z_i$, so
$$
 (I+(-1)^b U)Z^z=Z^z(I+(-1)^{b+p} U).
$$
An honest cat U report $b=p$ selects the positive underlying vector. The same argument applies after fault-free successful Z-only recovery. After an honest full recovery the underlying vector is already in the code, so the frame is zero. All identities hold termwise on an entangled input, including mixtures of previously dirty branches conditioned on the reported history.

For `ScreenCat`, a clean ordinary vote either projects to zero full-code syndrome or uses an honest unique-class correction before U. For a clean pre-screen vote (`ShortPreCat`, `LeanResourceCat`, `FilteredCat`), the same holds with the ordered terminal procedure. The first vote for all cats uses the screened source in place of a routine row. With no fault in that entire slot, including source activity, it is an honest U measurement of the encoded source. Three positive slots in at most five give at least one clean positive slot, with all operations inside recovery and all slot-boundary waits charged. The dirty-slot closure in §§2–3 preserves $B_f(M)$ thereafter. Early rejection on negative entry deletes accepting possibilities without modifying the physical prefix of any retained accept.

An exact end-to-end contrast is the unframed `ShortPostCat`. A single Z on D5 at the first shortened slot after a genuine negative entry makes the remaining physical U sign positive repeatedly. Without the XOR frame the controller accepts, its post-screen corrects the data error, and the decoded state is the genuine negative logical branch, with target fidelity zero. With the frame that trajectory rejects. Forcing two wrong positive reports on a true negative branch also rejects in the framed workflow. [Forced witnesses](forced_checks.json)

## 5. Output locality and screen placement

The ordered compact screen is Outer Z, Outer X, Inner Z, Inner X. It costs 188 CX and has the same physical program as the existing checked tree screen. On an all-zero branch its complete signed Clifford audit includes one inherited block plus one screen fault, every pair of zero-report screen signatures, and two inherited blocks. Across branches the input $B_f(J)$ is restored or rejected by the existing terminal replacement lemma. If a flag occurs, apply §3 and use the fresh screen followed, if necessary, by a full history. Correctability holds after the boundary for the same remaining global budget. In particular the screen output is in $P_f(J)$ after $f=0,1,2$ faults total: for one fault, either the input block was detected/removed by a clean screen or one zero-flag screen fault added at most one site. A nonzero branch with only that fault uses a clean reference with no remaining fault. For zero faults the screen is exact. [Ordered-screen lemma](../../docs/single-block-cz/components/single_block_cz_terminal_screen.md) · [Exact compact screen check](local_checks.json)

- **Post-screen cat.** Three positives leave $B_f(M)$. Apply the ordered compact screen after the last cat, including its bounded fallback. This gives $P_2(M)$.
- **Pre-screen cats.** Every slot that could produce the third positive starts with the ordered compact screen, using it as the deciding slot's own data check. After this screen the branch is in $P_f(J)$ for the number of faults through the screen. The finishing direct-cat instrument increases physical support by at most one site per further fault by §2. Destructive leaf readout has no data feedforward. The remaining boundary waits are single-site locations. From §4 the accepting trajectory has underlying $M$, so the output is in $P_2(M)$. A negative deciding vote forces a fresh screen before the next deciding vote. This explains why a distinct post-vote screen can be removed.
- **Matched five-parity baseline.** The exact check finds a counterexample to using the ordinary Z-then-X row as a terminal screen on arbitrary one-block input plus a further fault. For this actual controller, however, every accepting attempt reaches at least its third vote, index 2. By the end of index 1, a history with at most one fault has at most one physical-site residual. If that fault was in the source, the index-1 full row is clean and corrects or projects it away. If it was in a cat, a compact/conditional meter, a correction, or a wait, §§2–3 either leave at most one site or restore at a clean reference. If both faults have already occurred before index 2 begins, the index-2 full row is clean and removes the two-block input or rejects. If at most one occurred before index 2 begins, all later ordinary meter tails, admitted recoveries and cat instruments respect the site bound starting from the one-site envelope. In either case the last vote leaves $P_2(M)$, including its wait. The first source reuse and three-in-five logic are exactly the inherited controller.

These arguments certify accepted output. They allow rejection on a real magic branch due to fault location, decoder ambiguity, insufficient reported votes or unstable references. The holdout measures behavior with unrestricted fault counts.

## 7. Transfer to tree4

The cat's block-confined inner-meter idea transfers to tree4's regular nine-Z/five-X pass. `BareInnerTree` replaces only the five routine Z and five routine X compact inner meters in each ordinary slot. The original tree uses the same ideal bare generators already. Each dirty gadget introduces at most one block, including after conjugation by its leaf's V component. As in the repaired-tree proof, an honest bare inner-X on a bad Z sector only changes graph coefficients. The routine flags removed here formerly routed to recovery; a disappearing flag does not remove any of the parity/projector argument, and an invisible hook is now covered by the arbitrary one-block envelope. The original conditional inner meters, nested flag path, coordinate-aware repair veto, outer-Z flags, compact root and post-handoff screen retain their exact circuits and proofs. `FilteredTree` only prunes negative entry.

The pre-deciding cat screen relies on the finishing operation having a **physical-site** bound. Tree handoff with the single edge read is block-local. To try the transfer in the right data coordinates, the experimental controller runs V, the compact screen, V, then the actual four destructive edge reports and physical feedback, with the post-handoff screen removed. On Born seed 2 a single physical fault after the second-pass CX(A0,D6), with X on A0 and Y on D6, produces an accepted output with half its weight outside the radius-two syndrome lookup. The fixed GF2 fallback happens to decode it. A clean relocated run accepts, and the relocated controller accepts each of the four single edge-report-flip controls with decoded fidelity 1. Flipping just A0 and A1 at the actual handoff produces an accepted output with decoded fidelity 0.5. The two faults insert $U_1U_2$ after all screening; the repaired post-handoff policy instead rejects or correctly decodes this same injection. The site-bound violation needs one faulty location, and the decoded failure is minimal in the tested single-edge-report class on this seed. The experimental relocation and the unframed short cat are excluded from the proven candidates and holdout.
